      Refreshes every 10 min. Models from Open-Meteo (HRRR, GFS, NBM, ECMWF, ICON, GEM, UKMO), each scored on its day-ahead forecast for the last 7 CLI days and corrected for that bias; observations are KMIA METARs (T-groups and 6-h max groups); NWS point forecast; similar days from the IEM ASOS archive. Kalshi odds are pulled by a scheduled job about every 30 min (Kalshi doesn't allow direct browser calls). The CLI high settles off 1-minute data, so gusty afternoons can print above the hourly METARs. Pacing table, 1-minute spike stats and self-correction come from forecast-train.mjs (12 years of KMIA hourly obs vs the CLI, plus this page's own track record), retrained daily. wethr.net's Wethr High is pulled by the scheduled job when the WETHR_API_KEY secret is set.
