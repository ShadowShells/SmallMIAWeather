How this is built: every public Ambient Weather station inside the capture polygon keeps 5-minute readings on ambientweather.net. For the chosen day this page pulls each station's readings (plus the day before, for the bias history) and KMIA's METARs from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet, then replays the dashboard's own pool code from app.js every 5 minutes — staleness, range, gross-Δ vs KMIA, flatline and outlier QC, the hourly health check (bias + quarantine), and the bias-corrected mean and p90. Your block/allow choices from the dashboard are applied too. Stations that have since left the network won't appear, so a slot can differ from what the live page showed by a few tenths.
